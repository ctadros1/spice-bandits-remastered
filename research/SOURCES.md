# Original Game Research Sources

Checked October 13, 2026. Search aliases: **Spice Bandits**, **Spice Invaders**, **Spice Pirates**, `com.chillingo.spicepirateshd`, and App Store ID `467338170`.

The 1.14 executable and original data remain the primary mechanics reference. This catalog separates observed media, reviewed text, discovered links, and unavailable leads. The cited pages span different versions. A review date or video upload date does not prove the app version.

## Version and provenance exclusions

- [On5 company profile](https://at.linkedin.com/company/on5): corroborates developer identity; not game mechanics evidence.
- [Artist's Spice Bandits 2 turret post](https://alsukelis.blogspot.com/2016/07/some-turrets-from-spice-bandits-2.html): search identifies sequel-development art dated July 8, 2016; direct page returned 429. Exclude from the 1.14 clone. Possible later inspiration only.
- The current On5 homepage returned a gateway error. No authentic original gameplay source project was established from it.
- Unrelated Spice Girls games/podcasts, Dune material, Roblox tower games, and modern titles containing “bandit” were excluded.
