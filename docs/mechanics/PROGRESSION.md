# Offline progression and service replacements

Baseline1.14 has preserved tower/shop prices, campaign prerequisites, reward data, upgrade definitions and costs. It does not contain the former service's complete level/rank curve or promotion payloads. The offline reconstruction keeps those absences explicit.

## Traced and preserved rules

Kill XP follows native`addXP`: divide the score reward by10 and add/store with32-bit float rounding. Live credits, incomplete-battle exits and settlement are transactional. Settlement subtracts already-credited kills/score. Career bonus uses the victory-only integer rounding described in the baseline. Profile writes print full floating-point precision and restore XP to nativef32 after decimal decoding, preventing relaunch drift. Integer stock, spice, points and completion tokens remain unchanged by this normalization. The stored envelope hash, backup and newer-version protection are retained.

Victory spice uses`sp_first=3`, `sp_other=1`. Mode-specific perfect-game status awards a single star. Tutorial does not add normal career statistics or medals. Medal repeats/XP and secret predicates are covered in MEDALS.md.

Shop prices, level locks and previous-technology requirements come from the original153 offers. In-battle empty magic stock opens the recovered five-item offer and does not activate magic. A following activation first saves the consumed charge; failed saves grant no effect. Permanent tower technology costs spice, followed by a separate battle-money build/upgrade. Costumes likewise require an owned token before wearing.

`CMagicPanel` constructor0x96c60 grants five of all five magic items only in training. Fresh non-training profiles start with zero. Existing saves retain their recorded inventory; no blanket migration removes stock.

All ten upgrade levels cost30 points in total; minus refunds the full removed-level cost. Friend Power depends on the unavailable original friend service and is unavailable offline.

Continent access retains Africa's42-spice offer and free level16 offer. Compound prerequisites use native alternative/mandatory-positive parsing. Two malformed recovered prerequisite edges have documented virtual bridges in CAMPAIGN_ACCESS.md, without fabricated completion tokens.
