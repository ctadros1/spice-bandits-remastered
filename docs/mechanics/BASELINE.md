# Reviewed mechanics baseline

Reference: decrypted ARMv7 Spice Bandits 1.14 executable, SHA-256 `e3d230b954e1fb04e7120a20ed71df2cafaf4cd59cae73d1bf975427de7f6048`. Data is its bundled `offline.dat`. Derived repairs and candidate selection are recorded in `docs/content-audit.json`. Local assembly annotations and Ghidra pseudocode are in `traces/` and `decompiled/`; inferred types, SIMD expressions and calling conventions require review against assembly.

## Provisional adaptations

Local XP thresholds are `10*level^2`. The first observed promotion grants5 spice/3 points; later promotions use an explicit1-point/0-spice fallback. Fresh non-training magic stock is zero; the original training constructor alone supplies five of each item. Original prices and unlock tokens are retained. These provisional choices keep an offline profile playable while the historical service progression remains unresolved. No real payments, ads, unavailable friend services, or online rooms are simulated as functioning services.

## Native interface recovery

`tools/inspect_nib.py` independently reads the NIBArchive format described by [nibsqueeze](https://github.com/matsmattsson/nibsqueeze/blob/master/NibArchive.md), validating every section boundary and object reference. The importer derives menu geometry from the preserved TowerViewController.nib (480 objects). The original logo layers, gears, sky, tube, character stand and button order are rendered in Godot. The source canvas is768x1024; right anchoring and centered vertical cropping to the desktop1024x768 canvas are explicit adaptations awaiting equivalent original landscape screenshots.

`TowerViewController::viewDidLoad`, `0x1c8ac`, loads KautivaPro. `startGearAnimations`, `0x21ee0`, traces15/6/20-second gear rotations and a0.5-second spinner. `startLogoAnimations`, `0x20868`, traces the0.05-second rotating ring and0.3-second four-frame rocket cycles; logo rocking, eye tracking, randomized light and fly/bubble motions remain partial. `AvatarDrawer::sortArray`, `0x490f8`, sorts slot keys by length then lexical order, confirming ascending s1..s11 composition. Spicery's unavailable payment flow is represented by local balance and navigation to the original tech shop.

`IntroSequenceView::showChillingo`, `0x72170`, fades the original image in for1s, holds3s and fades out for1s. `prepareOn5SplashMovie`, `0x72db4`, selects rand()%2+1 and centers a600x532 video on iPad. `animateOn5Mask`, `0x72bf8`, waits until playback time5s, fades the player for0.5s, then `flyOn5Mask`, `0x729f4`, grows the original1024-square transparent mask to scale20 while fading for0.5s. The reconstruction retains these timings/dimensions and allows skipping. UIKit easing and global random sequence are not proven identical. The first-run streamed narrative request is bypassed offline because that clip is missing; publisher splash files are never presented as the story intro.
